Where each model runs
In the chat's own process or on a server, and which trained heads CLM loads.
What CLM reads to pick a route
It compares the conversation with each description. Reword one and you must train again.
What the language model reads
Its instructions once the route is chosen, and the only tools it may call there.
The details to collect
The language model extracts them over several turns. Code decides when none is missing.
Other questions CLM answers every turn
Yes or no, an ordered score, or a choice. These are not trained and not reviewed.
Answers become instructions
When a rule's condition holds, its text is added to the language model's prompt.
What the assistant can do, and what needs a yes
Your Python functions. A tool listed under confirm never runs without the user's yes.
What review mode keeps
Where verdicts are saved, whether a past route is reused, and encryption of the file.
